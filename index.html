nudge

Autonomous AI Gmail Follow-up CLI Agent

App Purpose & Functionality

nudge is an open-source command-line application that helps users manage email follow-ups efficiently. The app requests access to Google user data to perform the following core tasks:

  • Scanning Sent Threads: Audits sent Gmail threads to identify unanswered emails that require a follow-up.
  • Rule Filtering: Automatically skips threads where the recipient already replied or where emails were sent within 3 days.
  • Draft Generation: Generates short, natural 2-3 sentence follow-up replies and attaches them as drafts only inside your Gmail account for human-in-the-loop approval.

🔒 Privacy Policy

nudge operates 100% locally on your computer. Your authentication tokens, local state, and SQLite history are stored inside ~/.nudge/ on your machine. nudge never automatically sends emails without explicit human interaction.
