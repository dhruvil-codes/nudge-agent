🚀 Nudge — AI Gmail Follow-up Agent

Nudge is an autonomous CLI agent that audits sent Gmail threads and generates smart follow-up drafts using Groq AI (Llama 3.3 70B).

🔒 Privacy Policy

Nudge processes email data 100% locally on your computer. Tokens and database history stay inside ~/.nudge/ on your machine. Nudge never automatically sends emails—it attaches drafts to your Gmail inbox for human-in-the-loop approval.
